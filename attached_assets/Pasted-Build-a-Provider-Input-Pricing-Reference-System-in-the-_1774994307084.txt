Build a **Provider Input Pricing Reference System** in the RentZilla app.

The provider should be able to enter their own expected/service price while registering or creating a listing for any **service, profession, or equipment**, but this provider-entered price must **NOT be shown to customers**.

Customers should always see the **admin-controlled final price** from the Pricing & Commission module.

The provider price is only for:

* Admin reference
* Market comparison
* Negotiation insights
* Internal reporting
* Future pricing optimization

---

# 🎯 PROVIDER SIDE LOGIC

## 📍 WHERE TO ADD

During provider registration and listing creation flow for:

* Services
* Professions
* Equipment

Add field:

## 💵 Your Expected Price

Placeholder:

* Enter your expected service price
* Enter your daily/hourly equipment rate

Examples:

* Plumber = ₹300
* Electrician = ₹500
* JCB = ₹1200/day

---

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT RULE

This provider-entered price:

✅ Save in database
❌ Do NOT show in customer app
❌ Do NOT affect customer-facing price directly

Customer will ONLY see:

### Admin final price

from Pricing & Commission rules.
