FULL PRIVACY POLICY + REFUND &
CANCELLATION POLICY – RENTZILLA
 
1. Scope
This Policy applies to the RentZilla mobile app, website, provider dashboard, wallet, payouts,
customer support, marketing communication, KYC systems, and all global users including India.
2. Information We Collect
We collect account data, phone, email, profile photo, addresses, GST/PAN details, booking details,
location, payment references, wallet history, device identifiers, crash logs, KYC records, bank
details, and dispute evidence.
3. Why We Use Data
We use personal data for account creation, booking execution, fraud prevention, customer support,
escrow, payouts, tax compliance, analytics, legal investigations, service quality, and misuse
prevention.
4. Consent
By using RentZilla, users provide informed consent for account creation, OTP verification, location
access, booking communication, and optional marketing notifications. Consent may be withdrawn
by deleting the account or contacting support.
5. Data Sharing
We may share limited data with payment gateways, KYC vendors, cloud hosting providers,
SMS/OTP vendors, insurance partners, analytics services, government authorities, law
enforcement, and counterparties involved in a booking. We do not sell personal data.
6. Cross-Border Transfers
Because RentZilla may serve international users, data may be processed in India, Singapore, UAE,
EU regions, and other secure global cloud zones using encryption, processor agreements, and
access controls.
7. Retention & Deletion
Booking records may be retained for up to 7 years, tax invoices as required by law, and dispute
evidence until final closure. Users may request access, correction, deletion, or consent withdrawal
subject to legal obligations.
8. Security Measures
We use encryption in transit, secure storage, tokenized payments, OTP verification, role-based
admin access, audit logs, fraud monitoring, and secure image uploads.
9. Customer Cancellation
100% refund before provider acceptance. After acceptance, platform fees or reserved-time charges
may apply. Same-day cancellations may attract 20–50% deduction depending on category.
10. Provider Cancellation
If a provider cancels, the customer receives a full refund and may get priority rebooking. Providers
may face penalties, ranking reduction, payout holds, or temporary suspension.
11. Security Deposit & Damage
For rentals, escrow deposits may be held and deductions may apply for damages, extra usage, fuel
shortage, cleaning, or missing accessories after inspection.
12. Refund Timelines
Wallet refunds: instant to 24 hrs. UPI: 1–3 business days. Cards: 5–10 business days. Bank
transfers: 3–7 business days.
13. Non-Refundable Cases
No refunds for fraud, fake bookings, illegal use, duplicate claims, force majeure losses, or
user-caused cancellation after service start.
