## ⭐ 1. Reviews System (Provider Side)

### 🎯 Goal:

Providers should be able to:

* See overall rating
* See all individual customer reviews

---

## 🧩 Features to Implement

### 📊 A. Overall Rating (Summary)

Display at top of provider profile/dashboard:

* ⭐ Average Rating (e.g., 4.5)
* Total number of reviews (e.g., 120 reviews)

👉 Calculation:

* Average = sum of all ratings / total reviews
* Update in real-time when new review is added

---

### 📝 B. Individual Reviews List

Show list of all customer reviews:

Each review must include:

* Customer name
* Rating ⭐
* Comment
* Date

👉 Sort:

* Latest reviews first

---

### 🔍 C. Filtering (Optional but recommended)

* Filter by:

  * Highest rating
  * Lowest rating
  * Most recent

---

### 🔒 Rules

* Only verified bookings can generate reviews
* One review per booking
* Reviews are read-only for providers (no editing)

---

## 🗄️ Backend Requirements

### Reviews Collection:

* review_id
* user_id (customer)
* provider_id
* booking_id
* rating
* comment
* created_at

---

### Aggregation:

* Store/update:

  * average_rating
  * total_reviews
* Either:

  * Calculate on the fly OR
  * Store in provider profile (recommended for performance)

---

## 🚫 2. “Go Online” Button Logic

### ❌ Current Issue:

Providers can go online even without listings

---

### ✅ Fix Logic:

#### Condition:

IF provider has **NO listings**
👉 Hide or disable “Go Online” button

---

### ✔️ Implementation:

#### Step 1: Check Listings

* Query listings collection:

  * where provider_id = current user

---

#### Step 2: Apply Logic

IF listings count == 0:

* Hide button OR
* Disable button with message:
  👉 “Add at least one listing to go online”

---

IF listings exist:

* Show “Go Online” button normally

---

### 💡 UI Suggestion:

* Disabled state:

  * Grey button
  * Tooltip/message
