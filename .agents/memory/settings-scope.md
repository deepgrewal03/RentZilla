---
name: Settings scope
description: User-requested limits on the account and preference settings interface.
---

In Account Settings, keep email static with no editing option; only the name can be edited. Do not offer Login Activity, Currency under Language & Region, or Data Saver under App Preferences.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested these limits and removals.

**How to apply:** Preserve this scope across both Settings entry points and future redesigns. This is a Settings requirement, not a request to remove currency formatting from prices or email contact fields from support forms.
