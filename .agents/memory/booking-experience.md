---
name: Booking pricing and referral experience
description: User-requested navigation and Gold comparison behavior, and when referral invitations are allowed.
---

Keep My Profile out of the side-menu rows and Search out of the bottom bar; preserve avatar/profile access and home/drawer search.

**Why:** The user requested simpler navigation, not removal of account or search functionality.

Show normal and Gold booking prices. Strike the normal price for eligible Gold members; show non-members the normal payable amount and a gently pulsing membership-only alternative, respecting reduced-motion settings.

**Why:** The user wants Gold savings visible without falsely promising non-members a discounted charge.

Show the gift-themed referral invitation after successful booking confirmation, not after provider job completion or unverified payment. Close continues to the booking; Refer a Friend opens the existing referral experience with a return path.

**Why:** The approved interpretation of “finish booking” is booking success. Failed or pending payment must never appear successful.

**How to apply:** Preserve server pricing and existing discount eligibility; don't imply that separate ride fares have a benefit their server does not apply.
