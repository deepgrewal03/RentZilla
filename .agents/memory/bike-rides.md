---
name: Bike ride scope
description: Product meaning of Bike and its separation from rental bookings.
---

Bike means a motorcycle ride with a driver, like the existing car ride service.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose motorcycle rides with drivers rather than self-drive motorcycles or bicycles.

**How to apply:** Treat bikes as passenger rides with pickup/drop-off and matching motorcycle providers; do not offer their provider registrations as daily self-drive rentals. Keep passenger/helmet guidance visible.
